### Project Description

`A work in progress; currently private repository.`

The Turn-In-Time Analyzer is a Python-based tool designed to help educators analyze the submission times of student assignments. By connecting to Canvas and retrieving the submission timestamps for assignments, the tool generates visualizations that highlight patterns in submission behavior, such as peak submission times and late submissions.
