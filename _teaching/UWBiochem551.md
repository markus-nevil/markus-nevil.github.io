Biochem 551 serves as an introduction to modern biochemical laboratory
techniques and current biochemical literature for advanced biochemistry majors. Students attend a lab, lecture, and seminar each week. The topics covered in the lecture parallel the work in the lab, while the seminars focus on specific themes relevant to biochemistry research.
